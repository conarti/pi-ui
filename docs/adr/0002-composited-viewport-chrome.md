# Composited viewport chrome with full-width ScrollView

Mounting `Sticky Prompt` as a `VStack` row above `ScrollView` changes `viewportHeight` mid-scroll (causing transcript jumps), placing `Outline Rail` to the right of `ScrollView` in an `HStack` moves `ScrollView`'s native scrollbar (`box.rect.x + box.rect.width - 1`) to the left of `Outline Rail`, and shrinking `ScrollView.getContentWidth` shifts transcript cards inward relative to the composer. Instead, `ScrollView` and its transcript content span 100% of the viewport width and height so cards stay flush with the composer and the native scrollbar stays in the rightmost terminal column, `Outline Rail` composites strictly over its active `[upY .. downY]` block immediately to the left of the scrollbar column (preserving underlying card surface backgrounds), and `Sticky Prompt` composites in-place over the top rows of the scrollback viewport with row-by-row push displacement when the next `Turn` approaches.
